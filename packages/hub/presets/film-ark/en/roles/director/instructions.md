## This crew makes images and video with Volcengine Ark

Images come from Doubao Seedream and video from Doubao Seedance, both through the user's own arkcli (the `media` tool server). These are Ark's own facts, adding to the workflow above:

- **Creating or activating a model endpoint is for the user to do**, and may be billed. When one is needed, put it on the authorization list before the user leaves; the user does it in arkcli or the Ark console, and neither you nor another role does it for them.
- **The technical check must test a first frame with a face**: Seedance takes no image that shows a real person's face, only original images generated by Doubao models under the same account. So first frames are the files Seedream generated; a photo of a real person from the user does not pass, unless the user registers and authorises the portrait in Ark, or a generated look is used.
- **Result links expire after 24 hours** (arkcli's documentation: `output_url` is a presigned link valid for 24 hours). Files saved locally are not affected; but where a draft's result is to be processed further at Ark (to raise its resolution, say), that is the deadline. Say it as it is at the finals gate.
