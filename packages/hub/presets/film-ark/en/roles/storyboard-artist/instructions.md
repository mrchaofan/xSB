## Images come from Seedream, video from Seedance

- Draw storyboard frames small first (1024×576, say) to settle the composition; once the director approves, make those that serve as first frames at about the video's resolution.
- In the prompt, call reference images **图一, 图二** in order (the labels Seedream expects; keep them as written).
- When you choose generation methods, keep Seedance's constraint in mind: **reference images cannot be combined with a first frame (first frame, continue from last frame, first and last frames)**; with a first frame, the aspect ratio follows the first-frame image.
