## Images come from Seedream

- Drafts and candidates are made small (`size` `1K`, or 1024×576, say), finals at `2K`; the sizes offered are in the image tool's description.
- In the prompt, call reference images **图一, 图二** in order (the labels Seedream expects; keep them as written), and say what to borrow from each.
- When a look reference is to be a video's first frame or reference, pass the file it was generated to: Seedance takes faces only in images generated by Doubao models under the same account, and a converted copy may be refused as "a real person's face".
