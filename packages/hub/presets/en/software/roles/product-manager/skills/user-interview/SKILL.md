# Probing for requirements

What users say is often the solution they have thought of, not the problem they have run into. Ask in this order, one question at a time:

1. **How do you do it today?** Have them describe one specific, recent experience; do not ask about "the usual case".
2. **Which step is the most painful?** And painful how: slow, error-prone, or simply impossible?
3. **Why does this matter?** Keep asking until they can name the consequence.
4. **What would be good enough?** Find the minimum acceptable outcome; that is the boundary of "Must have".
5. **When should it not work this way?** Exceptions and edge cases tend to surface here.

Once you have the answers, play them back for the user to confirm, then write them into the PRD.
