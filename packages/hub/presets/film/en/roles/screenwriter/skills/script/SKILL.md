# Script

Register as `script/<title-or-scene>`, in markdown.

## Format

```
## Scene 3 - EXT. - FERRY LANDING - NIGHT

River fog drifts across the pier. A single light is on in the ticket booth.

A-Ning runs up dragging a suitcase, stops at the window, and knocks twice on the glass.

**A-Ning**
Is there still a boat tonight?

**Ticket Clerk** (without looking up)
Last one left at eleven.
```

- **Scene heading**: scene number, interior or exterior, location, time. A change of location or time is a new scene.
- **Action**: present tense, with a subject and a visible action. **One beat per paragraph**; the storyboard artist will turn each paragraph into one or two shots.
- **Character names**: the same throughout the film, and matching the names of the character sheet assets.
- **Dialogue**: short. Add a parenthetical acting note only when necessary, and write it as something visible ("without looking up", not "coldly").
- **Sound**: give any sound that matters to the story its own line ("A ship's horn sounds in the distance").

## Structure (short film)

1. **Setup**: who, where, wanting what. Establish it in a single image if you can.
2. **Confrontation**: the obstacle appears, and the character makes a choice.
3. **Resolution**: the situation changes. End on an image; do not sum up in dialogue.

## Required at the end of the script

- **Character list**: name, age and role, one-sentence appearance (the art director builds the character sheets from this).
- **Location list**: location, time, which scenes it appears in.
- **Prop list**: props that play a part in the story.
- **Running time estimate**: roughly how many seconds each scene runs, with a total that matches the brief.
- For a revision, add **revision notes**: what changed, and which changes affect art direction and storyboarding.

## Self-check before delivery

- Ask of every sentence: can the camera capture this?
- Delete all the dialogue: how much of the story can still be followed? Fill in what cannot with action.
- Does any action need a continuous shot longer than fifteen seconds? Split it.
